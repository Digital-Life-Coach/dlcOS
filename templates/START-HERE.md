## What this is

This folder is **your** notebook. It's a pile of plain text files on your own computer. Not an app, not an account, not a subscription — files. You can open them in Obsidian (the nice reader we set up), in any text editor, or on your phone. If every AI company disappeared tomorrow, these files would still be here and still readable.

The point of putting things here instead of only in a chat window: **a chat forgets, a file doesn't.** When you tell Claude something worth keeping and it goes into a file, every future conversation can find it.

---

## The four things to know about how Claude remembers you

Most people only ever use the first one. You have all four.

**1. This conversation.** What you're saying right now. Claude handles it. When you close the window, it's gone. That's normal and fine.

**2. What you've been up to lately.** A rolling summary of the last few months, kept in `Reference/Themes/themes-rolling.md`. It gets refreshed about once a month.

**3. Who you are.** The important one. Two places:
   - **Claude's Settings → Memory** — a short paragraph about you that Claude carries into *every* conversation, on every device. We pasted this in during setup.
   - **`Wiki/Knowledge/about-me.md`** — the long version. Your background, how you work, what you're doing. Claude reads it when it needs more than the short version.

**4. Your archive.** Everything you've ever done together — daily notes in `Reference/Dailies/`, older themes, older notes. You'll rarely read these. They're there so nothing is lost.

**The one thing you have to do:** about once a month, ask your coach (or Claude) to run the monthly review. It re-reads the last month, notices what's changed about you, and asks you to approve updates. Memory that never gets updated slowly becomes wrong — that check is the whole reason this works.

## Where do I put things?

| You have… | Put it in… |
|---|---|
| A thought, a link, something you don't want to lose yet | `Inbox/thoughts.md` or `Inbox/ideas.md` |
| Something you need to actually do | `Action/TASKS.md` |
| Something bigger with several steps | `Action/PROJECTS.md` |
| A "maybe one day" | `Action/SOMEDAY.md` |
| Notes about a person, a topic, a thing you looked up | somewhere under `Wiki/` |

**When in doubt, put it in `Inbox/`.** That's what it's for. Sorting it later is easy; losing it isn't recoverable. You will never be wrong for putting something in the Inbox.

You can also just tell Claude — *"remember that Ellen's birthday is in March"* — and let it decide where the file goes. That's usually faster than deciding yourself.

## When you're stuck

Being stuck is not a sign you're doing it wrong. This is a new way of working and it takes a few weeks.

- **Ask Claude directly.** "How do I…", "Where should I put…", "I forgot how to…" are all fine questions to ask it about your own vault. It can read this file.
- **Ask your coach.** Contact details are in `CLAUDE.md` in this folder.
- **Office Hours** — a standing group session, if your coach runs one. Bring the thing that isn't working.

---

## A few honest cautions

- **Nothing sends itself.** Claude drafts emails and messages; it does not send them. If something ever looks like it went out on its own, tell your coach.
- **Check anything factual before you act on it.** Claude is confident whether or not it's right. Dates, numbers, names, anything medical or legal — verify.
- **Your private things stay in these files.** Your health details, and anything someone told you in confidence, belong here on your own machine — not in the short memory paragraph that travels to Claude's servers. We sorted this during setup; keep it that way as you add things.
- **Back it up.** These are ordinary files, which means ordinary backup rules apply. If your computer died today, would this folder survive? If you don't know, ask your coach.
