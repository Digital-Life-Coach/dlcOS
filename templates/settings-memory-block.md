## What must NOT go in this block

This block lives in Claude's cloud-stored Settings → Memory and travels into every conversation you have, on every device. Keep it to durable facts about **you** and how you work.

Never paste in: your own detailed medical or mental-health history, anything a named third party told you in confidence (patients, clients under a confidentiality obligation, friends' health), or anyone else's identifying details beyond ordinary "my wife is named X" context. Those belong in your local vault if anywhere — see `/dlcOS:setup` Stage 4a for the triage.
