## Words & phrases to avoid

*The drafter runs a literal find-and-fix pass against this list before returning a draft, so be concrete. List actual words, punctuation, and patterns you never want in your writing.*

- **Em dashes (`—`)** — _AI loves these; many people find them a tell. Keep this line to ban them (drafter will swap in colons/commas), or delete it if you like em dashes._
- _Filler you overuse or hate: e.g. "actually," "just," "honestly," "really," "I think"_
- _Corporate/AI clichés to kill: e.g. "delve," "leverage," "in today's fast-paced world," "I hope this email finds you well"_
- _Anything else: exclamation-point limits, no emojis, no all-caps, etc._
